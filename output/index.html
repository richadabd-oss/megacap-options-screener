1. Dynamic Option Horizon (7d / 14d / 30d / 90d / 180d / 360d):

2. Volatility Edge (VRP):
VRP = Implied Vol (IV) - 30d Realized Vol (HV30). Positive VRP means option premiums are overpriced relative to actual historical stock movement.
3. Black-Scholes Greeks (Δ & PoP):
PoP = N(d2) for CSPs (probability stock stays above strike at expiry). Higher Edge Score = Higher reward relative to risk.
